    How it works

    The model reads. The maths stays in code.

    Claude does the reading and the design work. Everything that feeds the numbers is validated, calculated deterministically and open to change.

    01

    Ingest

    Files are read in your browser. PDFs and images go to Claude as they are. Word and Excel are converted to text first.

    02

    Analyse

    Claude answers through a strict schema. Estimated timings are flagged with their basis, and each step quotes its evidence.

    03

    Validate

    Output is treated as untrusted. Numbers are clamped, unknown references dropped and every fix is shown to you.

    04

    Calculate

    Savings are worked out in code from the step timings. Overlapping agents can never save more than a step's own time.

    05

    Adjust

    Change any timing, share or assumption and the totals update instantly. Export as a report or as data.

    Built by Ollie Wilson. Estimates are a starting point for discovery, not a business case. No client or employer data. View the source on GitHub
