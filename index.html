    How it works

    1. IngestFiles are read in your browser. PDFs and images go to Claude as they are. Word and Excel files are converted to text first.
    2. AnalyseClaude maps the process and proposes agents through a strict schema, so the output always has the same structure. Timings it had to estimate are flagged with their basis.
    3. ValidateThe output is checked before it is shown. Numbers are clamped, references to unknown steps are dropped and every fix is listed.
    4. CalculateSavings are worked out in code from the step timings, not by the model. Overlapping agents on the same step cannot save more than the step's own time.
    5. AdjustEvery timing, share and assumption can be changed, and the totals update straight away. Export the result as a report or as data.

    Estimates are a starting point for discovery, not a business case. The ranking uses hours saved a year per week of build effort, so quick wins come first. Built by Ollie Wilson as an illustration of how I scope AI deployments. It contains no client or employer data.
